Free online solitaire — classic Klondike

Roostr Solitaire is free classic Klondike you can play right in your browser: tap-to-move cards, one-card draw with unlimited recycles, free undo, and a daily deal that's identical for every player so you can compare times. No download, no account, no tricks.

How do you play Klondike solitaire?

Build the four foundation piles up by suit from ace to king. In the columns, stack cards in descending order with alternating colors, and flip hidden cards as they free up. Tap the deck to draw; tap any card to send it somewhere legal.

What is the daily deal?

Every midnight there's one shared shuffle — everyone in the world gets the same cards, so times and move counts are comparable. You can also deal a random game any time.

Is it free?

Yes — free to play in the browser with no sign-up. Your wins and best times are saved privately on your device.

How to play

  1. Tap a card and it flies to the best legal spot — foundations first, then another column.
  2. Build columns down in alternating colors; build the four foundations up by suit, ace to king.
  3. Tap the deck to draw one card at a time — recycle it as often as you like. ↩ Undo is always free.
  4. Play the daily deal (same for everyone) or hit 🔀 Shuffle for a fresh random game.

Empty column? Only a king may settle there.
