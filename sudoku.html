Free daily sudoku puzzle

Roostr Sudoku is a free daily sudoku you can play right in your browser: one fresh grid per difficulty every midnight, and everyone in the world gets the same puzzle. Pencil notes, mistake tracking, best times and a daily streak — no download, no account.

How does the daily sudoku work?

Every midnight there's one new puzzle per difficulty — Easy, Medium and Hard — and everyone gets the same grid. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1 to 9. Every puzzle is generated with exactly one solution.

Is the sudoku free?

Yes — free to play in the browser with no sign-up. Your progress, best times and streak are saved privately on your device.

How to play

  1. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9, once each.
  2. Tap a cell, then a number. Wrong entries turn red and count as mistakes.
  3. Use ✏️ Notes to pencil in candidates — they clear themselves as you solve.
  4. Three difficulties, one new grid each at midnight — same puzzle for everyone. Streaks are sacred.

Keyboard: 1–9 · arrows · Backspace · N for notes
