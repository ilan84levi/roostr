Free daily nonogram (picross) puzzle

Roostr Nonogram is a free daily picross: use the number clues to work out which squares to fill in a 10×10 grid. Every puzzle is generated with exactly one solution that pure logic can reach — no guessing — and everyone in the world gets the same grid each midnight. No download, no account.

How do you solve a nonogram?

The numbers beside each row and above each column give the lengths of the consecutive filled runs, in order, separated by at least one empty square. Cross-referencing rows and columns lets you deduce every square — our puzzles never require guessing.

Is the nonogram free?

Yes — free to play in the browser with no sign-up. Your best times and streak are saved privately on your device.

How to play

  1. The numbers on each row and column tell you the lengths of the filled runs, in order, with at least one gap between runs.
  2. In ⬛ Fill mode, tap squares you're sure are filled. A wrong fill costs a mistake and marks the square with ✕.
  3. Use ✕ Mark mode to note squares that must stay empty — it keeps your logic straight.
  4. Every puzzle has exactly one solution, reachable by pure logic — no guessing needed. Same grid for everyone, new at midnight.

Keyboard shortcut: X toggles between fill and mark.
