Free dice balancing game — tilt your phone

Steady Dice is a free physics game that uses your phone’s motion sensor: three dice sit on a tray, and when you move the phone, the dice move too. Keep all three balanced on the tray as it gets more slippery — your steadiest run is the score. On a computer, tilt the tray with the mouse or arrow keys. No download, no account.

How do you play Steady Dice?

Tilt your phone and the dice slide the same way. Keep all three dice on the tray for as long as you can — the tray gets more slippery over time, and your balance time is the score. On a computer, move the mouse over the tray or use the arrow keys.

How does the tilt control work?

The game reads your phone's orientation sensor and calibrates to however you're holding it when the run starts — so your natural grip is “level”. Your browser may ask for motion-sensor permission the first time; that's all it's used for, and nothing leaves your device.

Is Steady Dice free?

Yes — free to play in the browser with no sign-up. Your best balance time is saved on your device.

How to play
